Case Studies

A few examples of how automated reporting pipelines transformed operations, clarified decision-making, and eliminated hours of manual reporting.

Employee Utilization Dashboard

Click to enlarge image

Problem: All employee time data came from multiple unrelated software systems — PTO, labor hours, internal project time, and billable work — each using different structures, naming conventions, and reporting rules. Managers could not reliably calculate utilization because nothing tied together cleanly, and every report required heavy manual cleanup just to get a monthly snapshot.

Solution: Built a unified utilization model by:
• integrating PTO systems, labor-hour exports, and internal project logs
• transforming inconsistent fields, timestamps, and employee identifiers
• engineering logic to classify all hours as billable vs. internal
• creating utilization, billable ratios, and capacity KPIs

Power Query handled the semantic modeling, but the heavy ETL was automated through Azure Pipelines, which pulled, cleaned, merged, and delivered refreshed datasets on a daily schedule.

Result: Leadership finally has a reliable, real-time view of employee utilization — not just total hours, but how much time is spent on billable work vs. internal or non-revenue activities. Managers can spot under-utilization earlier, adjust staffing proactively, and eliminate the hours previously spent reconciling inconsistent time systems.

NPS Survey Analytics Automation

Click to enlarge image

Problem: The company collected customer feedback across multiple systems, including SQL and Cosmos DB. The data structure was inconsistent, messy, and difficult to interpret. Leaders had no unified view of customer sentiment and no reliable way to identify trends or drivers behind score changes.

Solution: Built a complete automated ingestion pipeline that:
• pulled survey responses from SQL and Cosmos
• cleaned and normalized the structures
• filtered the correct survey types
• transformed free-text answers into analyzable fields
• engineered NPS scores, sentiment, and segmentation All processed into a clean model ready for reporting.

Result: The company gained a real-time, organization-wide NPS dashboard. Customer sentiment insights became visible daily, and leadership distributed the report to the entire company — improving transparency and accelerating responses to customer issues. Manual processing dropped by more than 90%.

Sales Quota Performance Tracker

Click to enlarge image

Problem: Sales performance data was scattered across multiple systems — phone activity logs, CRM exports, quota files, and pipeline tracking tools. None of these systems talked to each other. Managers were relying on manual spreadsheets without a reliable way to see how daily activity connected to quota attainment or pacing.

Solution: Consolidated all sales activity sources into a unified dataset by:
• integrating phone-call data, CRM opportunity stages, quota assignments, and rep activity logs
• normalizing inconsistent identifiers across systems
• aggregating daily activity into rep-level performance metrics
• engineering pacing logic, attainment calculations, and forecasting KPIs

Azure Pipelines automated the ETL process, ensuring all sources refreshed together and fed a single clean model for quota tracking.

Result: Sales leadership gained a real-time, unified view of performance, enabling earlier detection of underperformance and more accurate coaching. Manual reporting was eliminated entirely, and quota pacing became reliable for the first time.
