HOW WE'VE HELPED CLIENTS

Small Retail Shop

The client was managing scattered Excel files & Word-Docs with no real structure. We organized their data into clean, standardized tables, making it easy to generate reports and dashboards. They can now track sales, inventory, and customer behavior efficiently.

Marketing Team

The marketing team relied on multiple social media data sources, making it difficult to see the full picture. We pulled and combined all sources into a single, unified location, allowing the team to easily view trends and correlations across platforms.

Finance Department

The finance department needed a better way to track employee utilization and billable hours across multiple departments. We created consolidated Power BI datasets, providing clear, real-time insights into team performance and resource allocation.

Help-Desk

The department manages both internal and external ticketing, with a data model spanning over 100 tables. Users previously spent hours just to gain insight from a single ticket. We built automated pipelines that consolidated all transformations, reducing the model to 5 tables. This created a single source of truth, enabling multiple new reports and actionable insights across ticketing.

Sales Team

The sales team relied on quotas from their reps , including metrics like calls made , videos created , emails sent , and first appointments booked. These metrics directly affected bonuses and tracked each rep's performance on the team. Existing Power BI aggregations were often inaccurate, leading to discrepancies. We transitioned these calculations into automated SQL scripts, enabling precise, reliable tracking and creating a staged system for consistent, dependable reporting.

Internal Team

The internal team initially relied on Power BI to perform all data transformations, which made models heavy and slow. We transitioned their workflows to an Azure cloud environment, creating a data lake and the first version of a data definitions table. With transformations handled in the cloud, Power BI models became lighter, faster, and easier to maintain, giving the team a scalable and reliable reporting solution.
