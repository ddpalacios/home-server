FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does landscaping cost in McHenry County?
Weekly mowing in Crystal Lake and surrounding McHenry County towns typically runs $40–$75 per visit depending on lot size. A full landscape design and install averages $4,000–$15,000, while paver patios range from $18–$35 per square foot installed. Irrigation systems start around $3,500 for small yards.
When is the best time to plant in Northern Illinois?
Crystal Lake sits in USDA Zone 6a (per the 2023 USDA map update — previously Zone 5a). The safe spring planting window opens after the last frost, usually mid-May. Fall planting (September through mid-October) is ideal for trees, shrubs, and perennials because cooler soil reduces transplant shock.
Do Crystal Lake landscapers need to be licensed?
Illinois does not require a statewide general landscaping license. Specific trades — irrigation contractors and applicators of restricted-use pesticides — do need state credentials. Many municipalities also require contractors to register locally and show proof of liability insurance, so check Crystal Lake's current contractor-registration rules at crystallake.org. Always verify registration, insurance, and request references before hiring.
How far in advance should I book?
Spring cleanups — mid-February. Summer installs — early April. Fall cleanups — mid-August. Irrigation and hardscape typically need 3 – 6 weeks lead time during peak season.
Can I water my lawn anytime?
Crystal Lake has water-conservation rules during the warm-weather months that include odd/even sprinkling restrictions tied to your address. The exact start/end dates and any morning/evening hour limits are set by the city ordinance — check crystallake.org or call Public Works for the current rules before installing or scheduling irrigation.
Do you charge to get matched?
No. Matching and talking to our AI advisor are free. You only pay the landscaper directly for the work you choose to hire them for.
