03 · Common questions

Quick answers to the doubts.

If something's not here, email us at support@palacios-solutions.com.

What happens after 14 days?
Nothing happens. Your trial line goes back in the pool, your demo gets deleted, and you're not charged. You only sign up if you want to keep going.
Will my customers know it's not me?
No. The voice sounds like a real receptionist who knows your business. We trained it on your website so it answers like one of your team.
Do I need to install anything?
No. We email you a one-click sign-in link to your dashboard. To put the chat on your website, you paste a single line of code — takes 30 seconds.
What if I don't get any leads in the trial?
We'll extend you another 14 days, free. No card, no follow-up sales call. If after that it still hasn't caught a lead, you walk away with nothing lost.
How do you handle customer data?
Your data stays yours. If you cancel or end your trial, every transcript, lead, and demo file is deleted from our storage. We never sell or share your data.
